INCIDENT REPORT — Operation NIGHTSHADE
Date prepared: 2024-06-08
Classification: Confidential — internal distribution only

Executive summary

On the evening of 2024-06-04, between approximately 22:15 and 03:40 local time,
unauthorized access was observed against the perimeter of the southern logistics
depot. No physical breach of the secure storage area was confirmed, but several
secondary indicators (disturbed gravel patterns near the eastern fence line,
cut zip-tie remnants on the perimeter sensor, and a 27-minute gap in the
camera-3 recording attributable to a deliberate IR-saturation event) suggest
a coordinated probing attempt rather than opportunistic trespass.

Timeline

22:15 — Camera 3 (eastern fence) registers an IR anomaly consistent with
        a high-output IR floodlight pointed directly at the lens. Recording
        becomes unusable for 27 minutes.
22:42 — Camera 3 returns to normal exposure. Two figures briefly visible
        moving south-to-north along the fence line.
23:18 — Perimeter sensor 7 (eastern, mid-fence) registers a tamper event.
        Patrol dispatched; sensor found with the housing removed but
        functional. No further activity recorded for 4 hours 22 minutes.
03:40 — Camera 7 (north access road) records a vehicle (dark-colored panel
        van, partial plate visible: ending in -47-) departing the area
        at speed.

Assessment

The pattern — synchronized camera obstruction, sensor tampering during the
obstruction window, prolonged dwell time, and timed exfiltration — is
inconsistent with vandalism or theft of opportunity. We assess with moderate
confidence that this was a reconnaissance operation conducted by a trained
team. The targeted nature of the IR saturation (camera 3 specifically, which
has the widest field of view of any single sensor) suggests the actors had
prior knowledge of the camera layout.

Recommendations

1. Increase patrol frequency on the eastern perimeter to 30-min intervals
   for the next 14 nights.
2. Deploy two additional thermal cameras to overlap camera-3 coverage.
3. Cross-reference the partial plate -47- against vehicles observed near
   other facilities in the past 60 days.
4. Brief the regional task force; this is the third probable reconnaissance
   incident in the area in the past 90 days.
