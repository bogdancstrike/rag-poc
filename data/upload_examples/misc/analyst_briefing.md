# Weekly Analyst Briefing — Week of 2024-10-14

## Headline items

### 1. Continued activity from the Echo Bear cluster

Activity attributed to TA-0018 (Echo Bear) increased measurably this week.
We observed three new C2 domains (`time-sync-7.net`, `dns-eu-cache.org`,
`update-services-eu.net`) all registered within a 36-hour window through
the same registrar that has historically been used by this actor. Two of
the three resolved to infrastructure overlapping previously-attributed
ranges within hours of registration.

Recommend blocking at perimeter and notifying sector ISACs.

### 2. New shell-company pattern in Cyprus filings

Building on F-2024-0814-002, we now count **23** Maltese-registered
companies sharing the Tower Road 142 address, up from 14 in August. Six
of the new entities have filed initial-period accounts showing real-estate
acquisitions exclusively in the Larnaca free zone.

The combined disclosed portfolio across the cluster is approximately
**EUR 184M**. Cross-referencing with land-registry data suggests at
least three of the acquisitions were from the same vendor entity
(`Aegean Properties Holdings Ltd`).

### 3. Maritime AIS dark events — possible new pattern

Vessel `BRIGHT-DAWN` (IMO 9901234) executed its sixth dark event in 90
days this week. Notably, the reappearance position this time was 187 nm
**south** of the typical reappearance corridor. This is the first
deviation we've recorded for this vessel.

Hypothesis: the operating cluster has become aware that the eastern-Med
polygon is being monitored and is shifting south. Requesting confirmation
from regional partners.
