Quarterly Threat Brief — Q3 2024

This brief summarises the most significant threat-actor activity observed by our team during Q3 2024. Three notable campaigns are described below, with primary indicators and recommended mitigations.

1. Echo Bear infrastructure refresh

Through the quarter, the Echo Bear cluster registered 14 new C2 domains across two registrars. Domain naming patterns reuse the time-sync-* and dns-* prefixes seen in prior campaigns.

Recommended action: block at perimeter; share IOCs with sector ISACs.

2. Glass Hornet npm supply chain

An npm registry mirror that was active for 6 days in early August served compromised @org/utils packages. Approximately 180 organisations are believed to have pulled the affected versions during the window. We are tracking 11 confirmed downstream incidents.

3. Maritime AIS dark events

AIS dark events in the eastern Mediterranean increased 40% quarter over quarter. Three vessels account for the majority of activity. The pattern is consistent with sanctions-evasion logistics rather than navigational anomaly.

Summary

Detection-side priorities for Q4: refresh Echo Bear hunting rules, complete the Glass Hornet retro-hunt across customer environments, and coordinate with maritime partners on the AIS findings.
